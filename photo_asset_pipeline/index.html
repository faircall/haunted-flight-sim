PHOTOGRAPHIC ART STUDY / FIRST PASS

Moonlit water temple

Photographs reduced to crisp pixels, preserving their shading and material wear. The roof uses photographic crops on a curved Blender mesh, baked to a sprite. The original scene stays available; this study uses the same 2D renderer and gameplay.
